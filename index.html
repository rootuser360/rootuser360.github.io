Mission

Our mission is to empower young adults through professional development workshops, access to community resources, and scholarship opportunities. We focus on creating alternative pathways for students of all abilities, especially those who have historically faced systemic barriers, regardless of their immigration status.

Nuestra misión es empoderar a jóvenes adultos a través de talleres de desarrollo profesional, acceso a recursos comunitarios y oportunidades de becas. Nos enfocamos en crear caminos alternativos para estudiantes de todas las capacidades, especialmente aquellos que históricamente han enfrentado barreras sistémicas, sin importar su estatus migratorio.

Scholarship Criteria

  • Must live in Tucson, Arizona
  • Must be registered to a college or university at time of applying
  • Must be able to attend Beyond Dreams course and volunteer time.
  • Welcome to apply: Daca recipient, immigrants, US Residents, or TPS recipients
